# The Digital Time Trap

## Overview
This project explores the **Attention Economy**, where digital platforms compete for users’ limited time and attention. The study investigates whether digital triggers, such as notifications and alerts, encourage users to remain online longer than intended.

##About Dataset
This dataset captures the delicate relationship between social media habits and mental well-being. It combines variables such as screen time, stress level, sleep quality, digital detox days, and happiness index. Ideal for regression, correlation, or mental health prediction tasks.

Researchers, psychologists, and data enthusiasts can use this dataset to study how lifestyle and online activity patterns affect human emotions and overall wellness.

## Objective
The main goal is to identify the point where productive app usage changes into **mindless scrolling** or passive consumption through statistical analysis.

## Research Type
This is an **Observational Study**, meaning user behavior is analyzed without manipulating variables or influencing outcomes.

## Key Concept: Zombie Ratio
We introduce the **Zombie Ratio**, a custom metric used to identify users with:

- **High screen time**
- **Low active engagement**
- **Frequent passive scrolling behavior**

A higher Zombie Ratio may indicate excessive or unproductive digital consumption.

## Dataset Focus
The dataset may include variables such as:

- **Screen Time** → Total time spent on digital platforms
- **Notifications Received** → Number of alerts sent to users
- **Active Engagement** → Meaningful interactions (searching, posting, learning, typing)
- **Passive Usage** → Scrolling with little interaction
- **Session Duration** → Time spent per app visit
- **App Opens** → Frequency of opening applications
- **Zombie Ratio** → Indicator of passive behavior

## Research Questions
- Do notifications increase total screen time?
- Is there a relationship between engagement and prolonged usage?
- At what point does useful activity become mindless scrolling?
- Can users with unhealthy digital habits be identified statistically?

## Possible Hypotheses

**Null Hypothesis (H₀):**  
Digital notifications do not significantly increase user online time.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):**  
Digital notifications significantly increase user online time.

## Methods
Potential statistical approaches include:

- Correlation Analysis
- Regression Analysis
- Hypothesis Testing
- Z-score Analysis
- Standard Deviation Analysis
- Mean Comparison Tests

## Goal
To better understand how digital platforms influence attention, engagement, and long-term online behavior.
